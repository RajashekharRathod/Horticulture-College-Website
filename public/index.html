About S. R. Patil College

S. R. Patil College of Horticulture and Research Institute, Badagandi is an institution focused on undergraduate horticultural education, practical skill development, research and extension. Affiliated with the University of Horticultural Sciences, Bagalkot, the college provides an academic environment where students can connect scientific knowledge with field-based horticultural practice.

Affiliation: University of Horticultural Sciences, Bagalkot.

About the Founder

S. R. Patil Education Foundation

The college's prospectus records that S. R. Patil Education Foundation established the institution in 2026.

A foundation for horticultural education

According to the college prospectus, the Bagalkot region and adjoining districts of North Karnataka have strong potential for horticultural development. The S. R. Patil Education Foundation established S. R. Patil College of Horticulture and Research Institute, Badagandi in 2026 to support quality horticultural education, research, innovation and extension.

The prospectus describes the institution's aim as developing competent horticulture professionals, promoting location-specific technologies, facilitating technology transfer and contributing to the sustainable development of the horticulture sector.
